# QUP product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Arabic-speaking students preparing for exams; administrators and content managers maintain reviewed content.

## Product Purpose
Start with a free mini diagnostic, understand measured strengths and needs, then continue an exam-specific learning journey. PRD.md and DOMAIN_MODEL.md remain the product authorities.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The current student app supports registration, login, mini diagnostics, preliminary results, session history and provider-hosted checkout. Full diagnostics, adaptive training and reassessment remain implementation work, not shipped capabilities. Each exam has separate paid access. Prices and access come from the API. No fabricated scores, activity, testimonials or mastery claims.

## Brand Commitments
The user explicitly delegated this redesign while retaining QUP identity on 2026-10-17. Preserve UI_BRAND.md's navy, blue, gold, Arabic typography, supportive direct voice and RTL layout. The final relationship between QUP and معين remains undecided.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Responsive mobile and desktop web, semantic navigation, visible keyboard focus, explicit form labels and errors, reduced-motion support, and text alongside status colors.
